Hi, I'm Lexi!

I'm a student at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts and Technology, with a particular interest in user expereince, interaction, and visual design.

I'm interested in understanding why things are designed the way they are, and how they could be improved. I'm curious, detail oriented, and drawn to the process of turning complex ideas into thoughtful, approachable experiences. My multidisciplinary background allows me to consider both the technical and creative sides of design, while constantly questioning and refining my decisions.

Outside of design, you'll probably find me hand crafting, playing badminton, photographing, or picking up another creative hobby.
