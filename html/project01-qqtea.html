01 / SELECTED WORK

QQTea.

Bringing a playful bubble tea brand to life through visual design and an online ordering experience.

Project

QQTea: A Fictional Bubble Tea Shop

My Role

UI/UX Design, Front-end Development, Illustration, Project Coordination

Tools & Skills

HTML, CSS, Visual Design, Interface Design, Collaboration

Context

IAT 339 / Team Project / 2026

01 / CONTEXT

Creating QQTea

QQTea is a fictional bubble tea shop created as a collaborative web design project. Our goal was to develop a distinctive visual identity and translate it into a multi-page website with a clear ordering experience.

The challenge was to balance a friendly, playful aesthetic with the structure customers would expect when browsing drinks, customizing orders, and moving toward checkout.

02 / DESIGN

Visual Direction

We established a style guide to keep the brand consistent across typography, colour, illustration, and interface components. I contributed to the visual direction and illustration work, exploring how QQTea could feel approachable.

03 / DEVELOPMENT

Designing the Experience

As the design developed, we translated the style guide into HTML and CSS. The website includes product browsing, individual drink pages, a cart, and a checkout interface. We also designed ordering options such as sweetness, ice levels, and pickup time.

These pages needed to feel connected, so we used consistent navigation, typography, and reusable interface patterns throughout the website.

04 / PROCESS

Iteration & Collaboration

Alongside design and development, I helped coordinate the team's progress by organizing responsibilities, identifying tasks that needed attention, and planning next steps.

As we moved between design and implementation, we revisited layouts and interface details to maintain consistency across the website.

05 / OUTCOME

Reflection

QQTea resulted in a multi-page website supported by a documented visual system. The project helped me understand how visual decisions, front-end development, and team coordination influence one another.

It was an opportunity to bring together my interests in illustration, interaction, and web design while working through the practical details of building a cohesive digital experience.
