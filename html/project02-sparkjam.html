02 / SELECTED WORK

Duolingo Storymaker.

Reimagining language learning through interactive storytelling and creative expression.

Project

Storymaker: Duolingo Feature Concept

My Role

UI/UX Design, Journey Mapping, Prototyping

Tools & Skills

UX Research, Interaction Design, Figma, Collaboration

Context

SparkJam Designathon / Team Project

01 / CONTEXT

Beyond Traditional Lessons

During SparkJam 2026, our team explored how Duolingo could offer more creative opportunities for language practice. While its existing lessons support vocabulary recognition and repetition, we wanted to explore how intermediate learners could apply their knowledge more independently.

Our team's research with 10 intermediate Duolingo users suggested that repetitive exercises could become limiting and that learners wanted more engaging opportunities for language expression.

02 / RESEARCH

Exploration & Ideation

We explored different ways to introduce creative activities into an existing learning platform. Through brainstorming and concept development, we considered how storytelling could encourage learners to practise vocabulary, dialogue, and conversational expression.

Our research and competitive analysis helped us identify an opportunity for a more guided, community-oriented language experience.

03 / DESIGN

Developing Storymaker

Our concept evolved into Storymaker, a feature that allows users to create personalized interactive stories in their target language.

I contributed to the UI/UX design, journey mapping, and prototyping, helping translate the concept into a more structured experience. The feature combines scene-based story creation, language practice, and community sharing.

04 / PROCESS

Feedback & Iteration

Mentor feedback revealed that our original concept needed a clearer creation process, stronger community engagement, and more consideration for content quality.

In response, we developed the scene-building approach further, clarified publishing and feedback interactions, and introduced ideas for guided vocabulary support and community moderation.

This helped us move from a broad storytelling idea toward a more structured feature with clearer interactions and responsibilities.

05 / OUTCOME

Reflection

Storymaker became a proposed extension of Duolingo that connects language practice with creativity and community participation.

The project strengthened my ability to connect research insights with interface decisions, respond to feedback, and collaborate under the constraints of a designathon.
