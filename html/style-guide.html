Ethos

My portfolio reflects my approach to design through a balance of simplicity and visual expression. A minimal layout and consistent typography establish clarity, while selective use of colour and imagery introduces personality. These choices reflect how I approach my work: understanding the purpose behind each decision, connecting function with visual communication, and refining the details that shape the overall experience.

How this translates into my process

01

Understand

Explore the context, people, and problems before developing a direction

02

Connect

Bring together user needs, functionality, and visual communication

03

Refine

Develop and iterate on details to improve both usability and presentation
